Cooking vs Coding

I’ve been obsessed with Ratatouille since I was a kid. It’s the reason I wanted to cook — and, I think, the reason I like making almost anything with my hands.

I do most of my thinking on the bike ride home from work. Today I had the soundtrack in my ears, and I started turning over how I’ve been learning HTML and CSS. Every tutorial, every course — none of it ever stuck the way it does when I just make the thing myself. I did this all through school: take something hard, turn it into something I could see, and suddenly I understood it.

Somewhere on that ride it clicked — building a card, in Figma or in code, feels a lot like cooking. You gather a few ingredients (a corner radius, some color, padding, a shadow) and combine them until it’s done. Pasta is the same: noodles, tomato, garlic, a pinch of salt.

Gusteau was right. Anyone can cook. Anyone can make.
