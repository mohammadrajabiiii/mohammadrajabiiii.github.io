Two Pages and a Chair

I'm back, after quite a good while since the last time I wrote on Medium.

A WAR happened.

And I think this is weird enough to say out loud in 2026. But that's how living in the Middle East is.

I am now writing on my own website. A place I've wanted forever. It feels like a new home.

Currently, only two pages are actually working. The others are mostly empty. It kind of reminds me of the first months after I moved into my own home. For a while, I didn't even have a chair to sit on. But it was mine, and I felt comfortable inside.

That's the same feeling I have writing here.
